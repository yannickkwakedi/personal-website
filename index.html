Background

Who I am.

I'm an entrepreneur at heart. Not by title — by instinct. I've always been drawn to the question of what could be built, and I haven't stopped asking it since.

What drives me is the intersection of innovation and execution. Ideas are cheap; the discipline to ship, iterate, and improve is everything. I build in public, learn by doing, and treat every setback as data.

I believe we're at an inflection point — AI is reshaping how businesses operate, and most small business owners are being left behind. Closing that gap is what gets me out of bed. Continuous improvement isn't a value I post on a wall — it's the operating system I run on.

I came up through healthcare administration — four years in a system where decisions have real consequences and execution is non-negotiable. That experience shaped how I think about building things that actually work in the real world, not just in pitch decks.

Now I channel that into building companies at the frontier of AI — with a deep conviction that technology should serve people, that Canadian businesses deserve a seat at the global table, and that the best time to build is right now.

Ottawa-Gatineau based. Always building the next thing.

AI Security
Protection

As AI becomes embedded in how businesses operate, the attack surface grows with it. The industry moves fast — security and responsibility can't be an afterthought. I build with protection in mind from day one: data privacy, access controls, and safeguards that keep businesses and their clients safe in an increasingly automated world.

Canada in the Arena
Mission

Canada has world-class AI research, exceptional talent, and a thriving SME economy — yet too few Canadian businesses are at the table of global AI innovation. The goal is to close that gap — one business at a time, one tool at a time — and position Canadian SMEs as genuine players in the global AI economy. By helping Canadian SMEs adopt AI, improve their operations, and build real competitive capabilities, we strengthen not just individual businesses but the entire Canadian economic fabric. AI adoption isn't a luxury — it's a sovereignty issue. The countries and businesses that move now will define the next decade. Canada should be leading, not following.
